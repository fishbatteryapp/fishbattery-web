Fishbattery acts as the data controller for personal data collected through the service.

What we collect

We collect account information you provide, such as email address, username, and profile data. We may also collect technical data such as IP address and usage data for security and performance purposes.

If you subscribe to premium, billing is handled by Stripe. We do not store payment details, only subscription status and related metadata.

Usage data is collected in an aggregated or pseudonymized form where possible, and is used solely to improve performance and reliability.

We only collect data that is necessary to provide and improve the service.

How we use it

We use your data to:
- Authenticate your account
- Provide optional cloud sync
- Manage subscriptions
- Improve product performance and reliability
- Prevent abuse and ensure service integrity

Legal basis

We process your data under the following legal bases:
- Performance of a contract (account and service functionality)
- Legitimate interests (such as preventing abuse, ensuring security, and improving performance, where these interests are not overridden by your rights)
- Legal obligations where applicable

Data retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. Upon deletion, personal data is permanently deleted or anonymized within a reasonable timeframe, unless retention is required for legal obligations.

Data storage and transfers

Your data may be stored and processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. This includes infrastructure providers such as Railway and Stripe.

When data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA), we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to ensure your data is protected in accordance with GDPR.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Request correction
- Request deletion
- Object to processing
- Request data portability

Signed-in users can use Account Settings to export their account data and permanently delete their account.

To exercise these rights, contact: support@fishbattery.app

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, such as Datatilsynet in Denmark.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not redistribute mods or paid third-party content.

Third parties

We use third-party providers for infrastructure and payments, including Railway, GitHub, and Stripe. Their respective terms and privacy policies apply where relevant.

Cookies and tracking

We use local storage for authentication, consent preferences, and core functionality. Third-party advertising scripts are only loaded after explicit consent. You can reject ad cookies and still use core website features.

Sponsored banners

Sponsored placements in the launcher and website are static and clearly labeled. We do not use autoplay ads or full-screen overlays. Premium and Founder plans receive an ad-free experience.

Age requirement

You must be at least 13 years old to use Fishbattery. If you are under 18, you must have permission from a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the website or application.

A history of updates may be available through our GitHub repository.

Contact
