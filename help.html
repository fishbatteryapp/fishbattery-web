Help & FAQ

Troubleshooting and common questions.

Common issues

Launcher won’t start

Try restarting your PC first. If the issue persists, reinstall the launcher and ensure your system meets the requirements. If you’re on Windows, SmartScreen may appear for new/uncommon apps — that’s normal for unsigned builds.

Mods not working

Most mod issues come from mismatched loaders or versions. Double-check:

  • You installed mods for the correct loader (Fabric vs NeoForge)
  • All mods match your Minecraft version
  • Required dependencies are installed

Instance crashes on launch

Remove the last mod(s) you installed and try again. If it boots, add mods back in smaller batches. This makes conflicts obvious.

Account / login issues

Log out and log back in. If you still have problems, clear local site data and try again.

Cloud sync not updating

Make sure you’re online and logged in. Sync can take a few seconds — closing and reopening the launcher can trigger a refresh.
